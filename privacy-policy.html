1. Introduction

CoinDojo ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our cryptocurrency trading simulator.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Display your profile and rank on the leaderboard
  • Track your virtual portfolio and trading history
  • Monitor app performance and fix bugs
  • Improve the user experience

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider. We implement appropriate security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Row-level security policies
  • Secure authentication

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication services
  • CoinGecko: Cryptocurrency price data
  • GeckoTerminal: Additional token price data

These services have their own privacy policies governing their use of data.

6. Data We Do NOT Collect

CoinDojo does NOT collect:

  • Real names or personal identification
  • Email addresses (anonymous auth only)
  • Payment or financial information
  • Location data
  • Contacts or photos
  • Real cryptocurrency wallet addresses

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please contact us at the email below.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Reset your portfolio and start fresh
  • Update your username and avatar

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the App and updating the "Last updated" date.
